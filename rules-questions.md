# League rules — what the site needs pinned down

Everything below maps to a setting in the app. Most have a sensible default
already in place; these are the ones where a wrong guess means recalculating
scores mid-season.

## Scoring

1. Confirm the points: bogey or worse **0**, par **1**, birdie **2**, eagle **3**.
2. Anything above eagle — albatross, hole-in-one — worth more than 3, or capped?
3. Is a double bogey scored the same as a bogey (both 0), or is there a
   maximum score per hole where a team should pick up?

## Team handicap

4. How is the team handicap set — a number you assign, or calculated from the
   three player indexes? If calculated, what percentages? (A common scramble
   formula is 25% of the low index, 15% of the middle, 10% of the high.)
5. Which index does it use — GHIN, the simulator's own number, or one the
   league tracks internally?
6. How often is it recalculated? Weekly, or fixed at the start of the season?
7. Is there a cap on how far a handicap can move in one week, or any
   adjustment for a team that repeatedly beats its number?

## Strokes

8. Are strokes allocated to the lowest stroke-index holes? What happens if a
   team handicap is higher than 9 — does it wrap around and give a second
   stroke on the hardest holes?
9. Does the tee played change the handicap? If one player is off blue and
   another off red, is there an adjustment for the rating difference?

## Tees

10. Which tee boxes are in play — red, white, blue, black? Any others?
11. Is tee choice up to each player, or assigned by the league?
12. Can a player change tees mid-season, and does that affect their handicap?

## Simulator settings

These need to be identical in every bay or scores aren't comparable.

13. Gimme range — what distance, and is it the same for every team?
14. Mulligans — any, and how many?
15. Wind, green speed, firmness — fixed league settings or default?
16. What happens to a ball that the sim reads as lost or out of bounds?

## Match logistics

17. Which nine gets played each week — is that fixed in the schedule, or do
    the two teams pick on the night?
18. Minimum drives per player per round. (Currently set to 2.)
19. What happens if a team shows up with only two players? Forfeit, play
    short, or use a substitute — and is there a handicap adjustment?
20. Are substitutes allowed at all, and do their scores count?

## Attestation

21. Who from the opposing team can sign off a card — any player, or the
    captain only?
22. How long do they have before it escalates to you?
23. If a card is disputed, is the photo the deciding evidence, or is there a
    replay/adjudication process?

## Standings

24. Tie-breaks, in order. (Currently head-to-head, then best single round.)
25. Do all 12 weeks count, or are the worst one or two dropped?
26. Is there a playoff or final week, and does it use a different format?
